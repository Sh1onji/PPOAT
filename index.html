The Seven Nations of Teyvat

Explore the 7 Nations of Teyvat

From the windswept peaks of Mondstadt to the bustling streets of Liyue, each nation offers a unique experience for travelers. Discover the rich culture, breathtaking landscapes, and unforgettable adventures that await you in Teyvat.

Mondstadt

Land of the Wind

Known for its freedom-loving people and breathtaking landscapes, Mondstadt is a haven for adventurers seeking the thrill of the open skies.

Liyue

Land of Contracts

Known for its majestic mountains and bustling harbor, Liyue is a land of commerce, tradition, and adventure awaiting every traveler.

Inazuma

Land of Eternity

Known for its electrifying storms and rich culture, Inazuma is a land of tradition, innovation, and adventure awaiting every traveler.

Sumeru

The Land of Wisdom

The city of scholars located in the west-central part of Teyvat.A fantastical nation of both lush rainforestand barren desert, where countless fruits of wisdom grow and are buried.

Fontaine

The Land of Justice

A terrestrial sea in the center of Teyvat. Following the direction of pure currents,crossing wilderness, the depths of the forests and vastness of the sea of sand, arriving at the origin of all thewaters of the continent.

Natlan

The Land of War

The nation of oaths situated in the western regions of Teyvat. Guided by the flame, you pass through lengthy gorges and valleys to reach the land where the torches blaze, where all fires converge.

Snezhnaya

The Land of Cryo

The nation of eternal winter in the north of Teyvat. Do not fear the bitter cold, nor shrink from the trials ahead. Face the snowstorms roaring across the sky, cross the silent and solemn frozen wastes and the cities thundering with steel, and press on into a winter untouched by the mercy of the gods.
