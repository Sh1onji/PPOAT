Liyue

A bountiful harbor that lies in the east of Teyvat. Mountains stand tall
and proud alongside the stone forest that, together with the open plains
and lively rivers, make up Liyue's bountiful landscape, which shows its
unique beauty through each of the four seasons. Just how many gifts
from Geo God lie in wait amongst the rocks of Liyue's mountains?

The City of Contracts

Liyue is one of the seven nations of Teyvat. It is marked by its emphasis
on "contracts. As one of the biggest economic hub in the continent of Teyvat which is why
people from all the seven nations flock in this nation to trade and participate in commerce.
