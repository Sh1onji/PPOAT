The Stadium of the Sacred Flame


Stadium of the Sacred Flame is a subarea and a main city located in Basin
of Unnumbered Flames, Natlan. It is considered the core landmark of Natlan,
and houses a continually burning fire called the Sacred Flame. The stadium is
the site of the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, a tournament in
which members of the six tribes compete to select the nation's strongest warriors.

The heat of battle never waver.
