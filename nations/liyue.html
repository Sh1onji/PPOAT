Liyue is one of the seven nations of Teyvat. It is marked by its emphasis
on "contracts." As one of the biggest economic hubs in the continent of
Teyvat, which is why people from all the seven nations flock to this nation

Liyue Harbor The golden heart of Teyvat's trade. Wander lantern-lit piers, haggle in the lively marketplaces, and watch merchant ships glide in from distant shores. Enjoy a feast of local delicacies at a teahouse , take in a performance at the opera, and visit the Northland Bank to see where fortunes are made. Time your trip for the Lantern Rite, when the whole city glows with fireworks and festive cheer. Order Now
Jueyun Karst Towering stone spires rise through drifting clouds, and winding paths lead to terraced tea gardens and misty mountaintop views. This is the place for slow mornings, fresh-brewed tea, and landscapes that look like a scroll come to life. Our guides will take you to the best lookout points and teach you a little about how the local leaves are grown and prepared Order Now
Qingce Village A calm, rice-terrace village where the pace of life slows to match the rhythm of the seasons. Walk the green paddies, meet friendly farmers, and enjoy simple, hearty meals made from ingredients grown just steps away. A perfect retreat for families and travelers who want a taste of everyday Liyue. Order Now
Guyun Stone Forest Ancient stone pillars stand tall above the clouds, each one steeped in legend. Visit at dawn or dusk when the light turns the cliffs gold, and hear tales of the heroes and adepts who once walked these heights. A must for adventurers, photographers, and lovers of local lore.
