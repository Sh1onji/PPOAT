Snezhnaya


The Land of Cryo


The nation of eternal winter in the north of Teyvat. Do not fear the bitter cold, nor shrink from the trials ahead. Face the snowstorms roaring across the sky, cross the silent and solemn frozen wastes and the cities thundering with steel, and press on into a winter untouched by the mercy of the gods. There, raise your eyes to the unblemished Pale Star. Though the extreme cold has long extinguished the warmth of bygone days, stand with those whose hearts still blaze with desire and sorrow, and bear witness as the old world burns upon the ice.
